# Image delivery specifications

Use sRGB PNG files. Keep important subjects away from the outer edges because responsive layouts crop differently on phones and desktop screens. PNG originals are welcome; they can be converted to WebP during the final deployment pass for better loading performance.

## Individual product pages

Each product gallery uses the numbered files that exist for that product in a 4:3 media frame. Do not create empty extra slides merely to reach four images.

- Recommended canvas: **1600 × 1200 px** (4:3)
- Minimum canvas: **1200 × 900 px**
- Keep the product or application subject inside the central 80% of the canvas.
- Use the same background, scale and camera angle family across a product's image set.
- Product video is hosted on YouTube and embedded by URL; do not upload MP4 files to this site.

Example names for Xtendra Bake Magic Plus:

- `xtendra-bake-magic-plus-1.png`
- `xtendra-bake-magic-plus-2.png`
- `xtendra-bake-magic-plus-3.png`
- `xtendra-bake-magic-plus-4.png`

## Segment images

The strongest result comes from two versions of each real photograph:

- Homepage segment tile: **1200 × 1600 px** (3:4 portrait). Keep the primary food subject in the central 55–60% so the narrow accordion crop remains useful.
- Segment listing and segment-page hero: **2000 × 1500 px** (4:3 landscape). Keep the subject near the centre with generous surrounding space.

If supplying only one image per segment, use **2000 × 1500 px** (4:3), centre the subject, and leave at least 15% breathing room on every edge.

Suggested stems:

- `bakery-tile.png` and `bakery-hero.png`
- `sweets-tile.png` and `sweets-hero.png`
- `snacks-namkeen-tile.png` and `snacks-namkeen-hero.png`
- `millet-nutri-health-tile.png` and `millet-nutri-health-hero.png`
- `ready-to-cook-tile.png` and `ready-to-cook-hero.png`
- `hotels-horeca-tile.png` and `hotels-horeca-hero.png`

## Homepage hero

- Recommended canvas: **2400 × 1600 px** (3:2)
- Place the food/manufacturing subject on the right half; the headline occupies the left half.
- Keep the image realistic and documentary in tone: natural light, real production context, no visible brands unless supplied and approved.
