FRYSOL®

Frying Oil Treatment for Cleaner, Better-Performing Oil

Discuss this application ↗Get a quote ↗

Overview

FRYSOL® is a frying oil processing aid and absorbent treatment powder designed to help improve the condition of used frying oil.

During repeated frying, oil gradually becomes darker, thicker and loaded with food particles and degradation products. FRYSOL® helps adsorb and remove unwanted material from the oil, helping restore a cleaner appearance and improve frying performance.

For manufacturers looking for an oil cleaning powder, frying oil treatment, used oil purifier, oil stabilizer, or solution for black frying oil, FRYSOL® provides a practical oil-management solution.

Bring Tired Frying Oil Back to Better Condition

Continuous frying changes the appearance and behaviour of oil.

Common problems include:

  • Dark or black oil
  • Increased viscosity
  • Suspended carbon and food residues
  • Smoke during frying
  • Increased oil consumption
  • Inconsistent fried-product quality

FRYSOL® helps condition the frying oil by adsorbing unwanted contaminants and degradation-related material, after which the oil can be filtered for continued use.

Adsorbent treatment is a recognized approach for improving the quality of used frying oils and can help improve colour and oil stability.

FRYSOL® + Filtration

FRYSOL® works as part of an oil-treatment and filtration process.

The powder interacts with the frying oil and unwanted material, and the treated oil is then filtered to remove the absorbed contaminants and suspended particles.

This is why FRYSOL® pairs naturally with an effective frying-oil filtration system.
