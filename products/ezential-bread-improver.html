Bread Improver for Better Volume, Dough Performance & Softness

Overview

Ezential® Bread Improver is a bread improver for professional and commercial bakeries, designed to improve dough performance, bread volume, softness and overall finished-product quality.

For manufacturers looking for a bread improver, dough improver, bread softener, bakery improver, or solution for better bread volume and dough handling, Ezential® Bread Improver supports more consistent bread production.

Where this product fits

IndustriesBakery
Solution familyImprovers & Enhancers

Better Dough. Better Volume. Better Bread.

Bread quality depends heavily on how the dough behaves during mixing, fermentation and production.

Ezential® Bread Improver helps improve dough performance and machinability while supporting the production of light, fluffy and consistently finished bread.

Key Benefits

  • Smoothens dough more quickly
  • Reduces fermentation time
  • Increases bread volume and yield
  • Improves dough machinability
  • Supports different types of dough development
  • Helps achieve light and fluffy bread
  • Supports excellent oven spring
  • Helps reduce dough wastage
  • Cost-efficient at low dosage
