JELUCEL®

Natural Wheat Fibre for Better Texture, Moisture Retention & Fibre Enrichment

Overview

JELUCEL® WF is a natural wheat fibre designed for food manufacturers looking to improve fibre content while also gaining functional benefits such as water binding, texture improvement and better product structure.

With a neutral taste and high fibre content, JELUCEL® wheat fibre can be used across a wide range of food applications without dominating the flavour profile of the finished product.

More Than Just Added Fibre

JELUCEL® wheat fibre is not used only for nutritional enrichment.

Its ability to bind water and support product structure makes it useful as a functional fibre ingredient in food formulation.

Key Benefits

  • Improves dietary fibre content
  • Supports moisture retention
  • Improves water binding
  • Helps improve texture
  • Supports higher yield
  • Can help reduce oil pickup
  • Helps maintain softness for longer
  • Supports better dough and bread volume
  • Helps reduce breakage
  • Neutral taste

Natural Wheat Fibre for Product Improvement

For manufacturers, JELUCEL® can serve two purposes at the same time:

Nutritional Function

Adds dietary fibre to the formulation.

Technical Function

Helps with water binding, texture, moisture management and product structure.

This makes it useful in products where simply adding fibre is not enough and the ingredient must also contribute to processing or finished-product quality.
