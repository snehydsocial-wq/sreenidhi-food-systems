Overview

Xtendra® Liquid Food Preservative is a versatile antimicrobial solution for food and beverage products, designed to help control microbial spoilage and increase shelf life while keeping the intended taste and aroma intact.

For manufacturers looking for a liquid food preservative, beverage preservative, or a solution to increase the shelf life of processed foods, juices, sauces and beverages, it provides a convenient solution across a wide range of applications.
