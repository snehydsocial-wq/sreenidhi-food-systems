NaSure® 19 is a rosemary and tocopherol-based natural antioxidant with a low-colour and low-flavour profile, designed to help protect foods, fats and oils from oxidation and extend shelf life naturally.

For manufacturers looking for a natural antioxidant, clean-label shelf-life solution, rosemary antioxidant, or natural alternative to conventional antioxidants, NaSure® 19 can be used across multiple food applications.
