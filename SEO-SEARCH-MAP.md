# Search Intent Map

This is an owner-facing record of the primary organic-search purpose for each deep landing page. It is not rendered to visitors.

## Query families covered

- Problem-first: `increase biscuit shelf life`, `control mold in cakes`, `prevent rancidity in namkeen`, `keep chapati soft for longer`.
- Ingredient-first: `calcium propionate for bread`, `sodium propionate for cakes`, `natural rosemary antioxidant for food`, `wheat fibre for bakery`.
- Application-first: `preservative for curries gravies chutneys`, `vanilla powder for bakery`, `frying oil purifier`, `commercial hot oil filter`.
- Segment-first: `bakery shelf life solutions`, `mithai preservative`, `HoReCa frying oil management`, `millet sugar replacement`.
- Product/brand-first: searches for Xtendra, NaSure, Ezential, FOSLIFE, FRYSOL, JELUCEL and Perfect Vanilla products.
- Education-first: `how to increase food shelf life`, `how to prevent rancidity`, `how to extend frying oil life`.
- Commercial/local: `food preservative distributor India`, `food ingredient supplier Hyderabad`, product availability and application-support enquiries.

Each family lands on the most specific useful page. Segment pages handle broad category problems, solution pages explain the technical umbrella, product pages answer product/application intent, and insights handle educational queries.
