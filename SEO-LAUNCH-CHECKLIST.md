# SEO and AI Search Launch Checklist

1. Deploy this exact folder at `https://sreenidhifoodsystems.com/` over HTTPS and verify every route loads without redirects or missing assets.
2. Add the domain property in Google Search Console using the DNS TXT record supplied by Google.
3. Submit `https://sreenidhifoodsystems.com/sitemap.xml` in Search Console and inspect the home page plus the highest-priority segment and product URLs.
4. Add the site to Bing Webmaster Tools; submit the same sitemap.
5. Keep `OAI-SearchBot` allowed in `robots.txt` so ChatGPT search can include the site in summaries and citations.
6. Keep business name, phone and email consistent across the website, Google Business Profile and relevant distributor listings.
7. Replace placeholders with real, original product/application images. Use descriptive filenames and retain the existing meaningful alt text when appropriate.
8. Review Google Search Console queries monthly. Expand a page only when real searches reveal a missing question or application—not by creating duplicate keyword pages.
9. Earn legitimate links and mentions from supplier partner pages, trade associations, customer case studies and relevant food-industry publications.
10. Treat `llms.txt` as a supplemental machine-readable summary. Crawlable HTML, internal links, canonical URLs and accurate structured data remain the primary signals.
